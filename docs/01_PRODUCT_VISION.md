# Product Vision

## Vision

Grow should become a trusted career discovery and direction system for people who do not yet understand what they are good at, what they genuinely enjoy, or which education and career path fits their reality.

The system begins with students from Grade 8 through graduation, then expands to adults who remain uncertain after education or several years of work.

The participant experience should feel like a thoughtful career consultant available through WhatsApp, not like a static test or a generic AI chat.

## Core problem

Many students are asked to make major choices before they have enough self knowledge.

Common examples include:

1. Choosing subjects after school
2. Choosing a college stream
3. Choosing a university degree
4. Choosing a skill to learn
5. Choosing between employment and self employment
6. Continuing a degree that does not fit
7. Entering a career because of family pressure
8. Following a trend without understanding the work
9. Staying in a field for years despite poor fit

The common failure is that the person is asked what they want before anyone helps them discover how they think, what energises them, where their abilities appear, what they value, and what constraints shape their options.

## Product thesis

Better career direction comes from combining self discovery with real world evidence.

Grow therefore combines:

1. Personality tendencies
2. Interest discovery
3. Strength and aptitude signals
4. Work values
5. Preferred environment
6. Education level
7. Family and financial constraints
8. Access to devices and learning resources
9. Geographic context
10. Career market reality
11. Practical field experiments
12. Follow up evidence

## Primary outcome

A participant should leave with more than a personality description.

They should understand:

1. Their strongest current signals
2. Which career clusters appear compatible
3. Why those paths fit
4. Which paths appear less suitable and why
5. What tradeoffs matter
6. What education or skill route is required
7. What earning opportunities may exist
8. What small experiment to try next
9. What decision can reasonably be made now
10. What remains uncertain

## Product promise

Grow helps a participant move from confusion to evidence based direction.

It does not promise perfect prediction.

It reduces uncertainty through structured discovery, practical experimentation, and realistic guidance.

## Product personality

Participant facing communication should be:

1. Respectful
2. Clear
3. Simple
4. Non judgemental
5. Curious
6. Practical
7. Honest about uncertainty
8. Appropriate for Pakistani cultural context
9. Primarily in natural Urdu

## Long term vision

A mature Grow system should be able to:

1. Recruit and onboard participants through WhatsApp
2. Conduct adaptive discovery conversations
3. Run structured assessments
4. Detect missing or conflicting evidence
5. Assign practical field experiments
6. Produce ranked career directions
7. Explain the reasoning in understandable Urdu
8. Compare learning cost, time, risk, and earning potential
9. Build an education and skills roadmap
10. Track participant progress
11. Detect when recommendations appear wrong
12. Learn from outcomes
13. Support career switching adults
14. Support parents without allowing parents to replace the participant voice
15. Expand across Pakistan while retaining local context

## Geographic vision

Phase 1: Karachi

Phase 2: Sindh and Punjab

Phase 3: broader Pakistan

Expansion should not be treated only as marketing expansion. Regional language, education access, family structures, internet access, local labour markets, gender constraints, and opportunity differences must be reflected in the product.

## Business model direction

Initial access model:

1. Standard participant fee: PKR 500
2. Reduced participant fee: PKR 200 when full payment is difficult
3. Free access for genuinely deserving participants after human review

Long term possibilities may include:

1. Direct participant services
2. School partnerships
3. College partnerships
4. Training institute partnerships
5. NGO and youth programme partnerships
6. Career counsellor tooling
7. White label institutional deployments

These are later possibilities, not MVP requirements.

## Non goals

Grow should not become:

1. A generic tutoring chatbot
2. A motivational quote service
3. A one score career test
4. A course recommendation affiliate engine
5. A system that pushes participants toward whatever career currently trends online
6. A replacement for qualified mental health care
7. A system that judges capability from income, gender, English fluency, or school brand
8. A fully autonomous system for sensitive decisions involving minors

## Defensible advantage

The strongest long term advantage is not the chat interface.

It is the accumulated career discovery intelligence built from:

1. Structured participant evidence
2. Repeated assessment patterns
3. Practical trial outcomes
4. Recommendation outcomes
5. Pakistan specific career opportunity knowledge
6. Regional differences
7. Longitudinal participant progress
8. Human reviewer corrections
9. Carefully promoted knowledge rules

## North star

Grow is successful when a participant who begins with I do not know what I should do can eventually say:

I understand myself better, I know which directions deserve serious attention, I know why, and I know what to do next.
