# Fees and Access

## Purpose

Grow should remain affordable without turning free access into the default expectation.

The access model should preserve both dignity and seriousness.

## Initial fee structure

1. Standard fee: PKR 500
2. Reduced fee: PKR 200 when full payment is genuinely difficult
3. Free access: available when the participant is genuinely unable to pay and would otherwise be excluded

## Principle

The system should distinguish between inability to pay and unwillingness to pay.

That distinction cannot be made reliably from one automated question.

## Suggested access review

Collect only enough information to understand affordability.

Potential questions:

1. Are you currently earning
2. Who currently pays for your education
3. Is PKR 500 difficult, impossible, or manageable
4. Would PKR 200 be manageable
5. Are there immediate family or education expenses affecting payment
6. Is there any other support available

Questions should be phrased respectfully and kept minimal.

## Decision model

Possible recommendations:

1. Standard fee appropriate
2. Reduced fee appropriate
3. Free access likely appropriate
4. Human review required

The final reduced or free decision should remain human reviewed, especially during the pilot.

## Dignity rules

Do not:

1. Demand humiliating proof for small support amounts
2. Publicly label participants by payment category
3. Treat free participants as less serious
4. Expose financial information to other students
5. Use financial difficulty as a proxy for capability

## Seriousness rule

Payment is not the only signal of commitment.

A free participant who completes every task may be more serious than a paying participant who repeatedly disengages.

Seriousness should be measured through behaviour such as:

1. Response consistency
2. Assessment completion
3. Practical task completion
4. Attendance where relevant
5. Follow through

## Payment workflow

A future automated flow may include:

1. Fee category decision
2. Payment instructions
3. Payment evidence upload
4. Operator verification where required
5. Receipt or confirmation
6. Outstanding balance status
7. Reminder

## Review cycle

The pricing model is experimental.

Track:

1. Completion by fee category
2. Dropout by fee category
3. Payment friction
4. Participant value perception
5. Operator cost per participant
6. Support fund requirement

Do not assume the first pricing structure is economically sustainable until real pilot data exists.
