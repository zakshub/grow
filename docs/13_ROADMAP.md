# Product Roadmap

## Roadmap principle

Grow should scale capability in the same order that evidence matures.

Do not scale acquisition, automation, or geography faster than recommendation quality.

## Phase 0: Foundation

Goal: define the system before building it.

Deliverables:

1. Product vision
2. Audience definition
3. Career discovery model
4. Assessment model
5. Participant journey
6. WhatsApp operating model
7. Recruitment model
8. Fee model
9. Privacy and safety rules
10. Technical architecture
11. Automation boundaries
12. MVP plan
13. Measurement framework
14. Research standards

Exit condition:

The team can explain exactly what Grow does, for whom, how it reaches a recommendation, and what remains human.

## Phase 1: Manual assisted discovery pilot

Goal: validate the career guidance method with real people before heavy automation.

Scope:

1. Karachi
2. Urdu
3. Small warm network cohort
4. Grade 8 through graduation as primary audience
5. Human reviewed recommendations
6. Simple data capture
7. Manual or semi automated WhatsApp operations where required

Test:

1. Do participants understand the offer
2. Do questions reveal useful differences
3. Which questions fail
4. Do practical tasks improve confidence
5. Do recommendations feel specific rather than generic
6. Can participants explain the result back accurately

Exit condition:

Repeated evidence that the method helps participants move from confusion to clearer direction.

## Phase 2: WhatsApp MVP

Goal: automate the repeatable parts of the validated pilot.

Build:

1. Official WhatsApp integration
2. Participant state machine
3. Consent flow
4. Adaptive discovery conversation
5. Core assessment
6. Evidence store
7. Practical experiment workflow
8. Human review queue
9. Recommendation output
10. Fee status
11. Seven day and thirty day follow up
12. Basic analytics

Exit condition:

The system can run a meaningful participant journey with humans focused mainly on review and exceptions.

## Phase 3: Career intelligence expansion

Goal: improve depth and recommendation realism.

Build:

1. Broader career taxonomy
2. Career trait profiles
3. Education pathway data
4. Pakistan opportunity research
5. Remote work opportunity layer
6. Earning evidence with freshness and confidence
7. Larger practical experiment library
8. Better contradiction handling
9. Career switching track

Exit condition:

Recommendations include strong self fit reasoning plus credible route and opportunity information.

## Phase 4: Sindh and Punjab expansion

Goal: test whether Grow survives regional variation.

Build:

1. Regional acquisition experiments
2. Language support where necessary
3. Regional education pathways
4. Local career opportunity evidence
5. Female participant trust patterns
6. Guardian flow improvements
7. Low bandwidth and voice heavy experience

Exit condition:

The product performs acceptably across multiple cities and participant backgrounds without relying on Karachi assumptions.

## Phase 5: Automation maturity

Goal: reduce manual operating cost while preserving quality.

Automate further:

1. Evidence extraction
2. Question selection
3. Experiment selection
4. Recommendation drafting
5. Follow up classification
6. Referral tracking
7. Operator summaries
8. Weekly learning observations
9. Market evidence refresh tasks

Keep human review for sensitive cases and uncertain recommendations.

Exit condition:

Human time is concentrated on cases where human judgement materially improves the result.

## Phase 6: Longitudinal guidance

Goal: become useful after the first recommendation.

Build:

1. Personal roadmap tracking
2. Skill progress
3. Education milestone tracking
4. Career decision check ins
5. Recommendation revision
6. Portfolio and project prompts
7. Internship and entry route guidance
8. Career change planning

Exit condition:

Grow can support a person across multiple career decisions rather than one assessment event.

## Phase 7: National product

Goal: serve broader Pakistan with strong local context.

Potential capabilities:

1. Regional languages
2. Pakistan wide career and education knowledge
3. Institutional partnerships
4. School and college programmes
5. NGO deployments
6. Counsellor tools
7. Parent guidance experiences
8. Alumni and mentor network

Exit condition:

National expansion does not materially reduce recommendation quality or participant trust.

## Phase 8: Institutional and commercial platform

Only after the direct participant product is proven.

Potential products:

1. School career discovery platform
2. College guidance platform
3. Youth programme platform
4. Career counsellor workspace
5. White label deployment
6. Organisational talent discovery variant

These directions require separate validation and should not contaminate the student MVP prematurely.

## Roadmap dependencies

Assessment quality before recommendation automation.

Recommendation quality before acquisition scale.

Privacy and minor safety before school scale.

Local evidence before national claims.

Outcome data before advanced predictive models.

Operational proof before institutional sales.

## Roadmap warning

The biggest risk is building a technically impressive automated career system before proving that its questions, evidence, and recommendations are actually better than a thoughtful human conversation.
