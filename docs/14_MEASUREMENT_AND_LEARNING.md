# Measurement and Learning

## Principle

Grow should measure whether it creates better direction, not merely whether people reply to messages.

## Measurement layers

### Acquisition metrics

1. People approached
2. People who initiate WhatsApp conversation
3. Source by participant
4. Response rate
5. Cost per interested participant where relevant
6. Referral rate

### Onboarding metrics

1. Consent completion
2. Profile completion
3. Time to profile completion
4. Dropout point
5. Minor guardian completion rate

### Discovery metrics

1. Discovery conversation completion
2. Average number of questions
3. Clarification frequency
4. Voice versus text usage
5. Contradiction frequency
6. Participant reported fatigue

### Assessment metrics

1. Assessment completion
2. Time to completion
3. Question skip rate
4. Dimension confidence
5. Human disagreement rate
6. Repeated question patterns associated with dropout

### Experiment metrics

1. Experiment assignment
2. Completion rate
3. Time to completion
4. Self reported enjoyment
5. Reviewer quality assessment
6. Recommendation change after experiment

A strong experiment should add information, not merely occupy the participant.

### Recommendation quality metrics

1. Participant understanding
2. Participant relevance rating
3. Human reviewer agreement
4. Recommendation diversity
5. Confidence calibration
6. Percentage of recommendations materially changed by human review
7. Participant ability to explain why a recommended path fits

### Action metrics

1. Next step chosen
2. Seven day action completion
3. Thirty day action completion
4. Skill exploration started
5. Course or subject research completed
6. Practical project started
7. Mentor or professional contacted

### Longer term outcome metrics

When consent and operational capacity allow:

1. Subject choice
2. Degree choice
3. Skill completion
4. Internship
5. Employment
6. Career switch
7. Continued satisfaction
8. Recommendation regret
9. Recommendation revision

## Quality over conversion

A high completion rate is not automatically good.

If the system makes the assessment too easy and generic, more people may finish but receive less useful guidance.

Track completion together with recommendation quality.

## Source quality

For each acquisition source track:

1. Leads
2. Started
3. Completed assessment
4. Completed experiment
5. Received recommendation
6. Took action
7. Referred others

This reveals which networks produce engaged participants rather than raw volume.

## Fee metrics

Track separately by fee category:

1. Standard
2. Reduced
3. Free

Measure:

1. Completion
2. Engagement
3. Follow through
4. Dropout
5. Operator cost

Do not use fee category as a capability score.

## Fairness checks

Regularly compare outcomes across relevant groups where ethically and legally appropriate.

Potential dimensions:

1. Gender
2. Education level
3. Government versus private education background
4. Region
5. Language preference
6. Device access
7. Fee category

The goal is to detect system bias, not to create stereotypes.

## Recommendation distribution checks

Monitor whether the system over recommends a narrow group of fashionable fields.

Warning signals:

1. Technology recommendations dominate without evidence
2. Gender correlates suspiciously with career category
3. Low English fluency suppresses high skill recommendations
4. One personality pattern always maps to one career
5. The same recommendation appears for very different participants

## Human correction learning

Every human override should record:

1. What the system proposed
2. What the human changed
3. Why
4. Whether the participant outcome later supported the change

Repeated corrections are a major source of product learning.

## Weekly learning report

Generate a weekly summary covering:

1. Funnel changes
2. Main dropout points
3. Most common unresolved confusion
4. Human override patterns
5. Weak assessment questions
6. Strong and weak experiments
7. Source quality
8. Geographic differences
9. Participant feedback
10. Candidate hypotheses for the product brain

## Knowledge promotion

Weekly reports generate observations.

Repeated observations may become hypotheses.

Only reviewed, repeated, well supported hypotheses should become promoted rules.

## North star measurement

The most important result is not number of assessments.

A stronger north star is the proportion of participants who move from unclear direction to a specific, understood, evidence supported next action and then actually attempt that action.
